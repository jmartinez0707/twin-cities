Adopt don't Shop

Primarly finding home for our precious animals is what we'd like. If unable to adopt there are multiple other ways of helping such as, fostering and volunteering.

Our Adoption Process

Thank you for your interest in adopting a rescue animal from us!

Once you complete a formal application on our website, a volunteer will reach out to you for a phone interview. As part of the application process, please note that an in person home visit from a volunteer is required. If we feel you may be a good fit for the animal you applied for, we will then set up a meeting with the animal you've chosen. After the meeting, all parties will decide whether to follow through with an adoption.

  1. Browse: learn more about our animals.
  2. Apply: short application to learn about you and your home.
  3. Contact Us: let us know what you're looking for. We're happy to help.
  4. Complete: finish paperwork needed for in home visit.
  5. Family Grew: now that you've taken in an animal. You'll find more joy in life.

Fostering

We rely on foster homes to house all of our animals in need. Each foster home has a team member as a mentor to guide them through the process. The mentor for that animal interviews potential adopters and ultimately chooses the animal's forever home. The mentor will also decide and facilitate all vetting and veterinary appointments. We cover the cost of all vetting, we just ask that you get the foster animal to their appointments. Foster homes typically supply food for the animal though we can assist if needed. Foster homes are encouraged to promote their animal for adoption through word of mouth and/or social media.

We Provide

  • Food (if needed)
  • Supplies (if needed)
  • All vetting expenses paid for

You Provide

  • A loving home for the foster animal
  • Training and socializing of animals
  • Trips to veterinary appointments
  • Bringing them in for grooming
