All Animals are our Forever Friends

We here at Twin Cities Animal Rescue are rescuing, rehabing, and rehoming animals in need. We rely on our community for support by giving animals a second chance.

Begin helping today

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue abandoned, abused, and neglected animals by providing them with loving foster care and medical treatment, while trying to match them with lifelong, dedicated and loving families.

Our Impact

We're thankful for our community, which includes, volunteers, fosters, donors, and our local veterinians offices.

  • 10,000+ Animals Rescued since 1969
  • 250+ Animals Adopted in 2025
  • $200,000+ Animals Medical expenses in 2025
  • 55 Years of Compassion for Animals

How You Can Help

There are multiple ways to help. Please consider volunteering, fostering or donating supplies or money to our organization.

  • Volunteer Walk dogs, clean cages, socialize cats or help at fundraising events. View volunteer opportunities
  • Foster Take an animal into your home temporarily. Fostering frees up space at our rescue and help them acclimate to living with a family. View foster opportunities
  • Adopt Taking in an animal to become a part of your family is wonderful. View adoption opportunities
  • Donate Donating money and supplies helps in taking care of our animals. Contact us about donating
