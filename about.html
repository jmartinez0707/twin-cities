A little bit about us

We are an animal rescue organization that has been operating since 1969. Our members and volunteers devote their time and efforts into rescuing, rehabilitating and rehoming unwanted, stray and abandoned animals. We had no paid employees and receive no state funding. We exist solely on donations and money made from fundraisers. All animals in our care are safely cared for in foster homes throughout our community and given care from local veterinarians. We take pride in our work and in makinga difference in the lives of rescue animals.

What We Believe

Every animal that comes through our doors gets a chance. We believe in second chances, not second guessing. Every potential adopted goes through a thoughtful application and in person interview. Our goal is to set each animal and family up for success, so every adoption is truly forever. If for any reason an adoption doesn't work, the animal comes back to us. No animal is abandoned for a second time.
